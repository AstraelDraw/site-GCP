"You can’t change who you are. But you can change what you do." - Fitz Vacker

Keeper of the Lost Cities is a fantasy series written by Shannon Messenger, which immerses its readers in a magical world filled with elves, fantastic creatures, and ancient mysteries. The story follows Sophie Foster, a young girl with exceptional powers, who discovers that she belongs to a hidden world where lost cities, secrets, and conspiracies disrupt the fragile balance between different races. Through thrilling adventures, Sophie and her friends must navigate betrayals, alliances, and dangers to save their world while learning to master their powers. The universe of Keeper of the Lost Cities is rich with magic, political intrigue, and explorations of deep themes such as identity, sacrifice, and courage.

An overview of each volume:

  • Volume 1:

    Sophie Foster, 12 years old, is not like other teenagers. She hears people’s thoughts and feels out of place, even within her own family. But one day, she discovers that she belongs to a hidden world and has never truly been where she belongs among humans. In the Lost Cities, she learns that she possesses rare abilities and must juggle her new learning with ancient secrets. But when danger looms, Sophie will have to choose between staying in the shadows or facing the truth.

  • Volume 2: Exile

    Sophie Foster is no longer alone. In the Lost Cities, she has finally found people like her with extraordinary abilities. But the mysteries surrounding her past continue to haunt her, and an even greater danger seems to loom over her and her loved ones. As a secret about her power emerges, Sophie must confront impossible choices, and her journey could take her to places she never thought possible.

  • Volume 3: Everblaze

    Sophie Foster thought the truth about her past would bring answers, but it only raises more questions. As her powers grow and become more unstable, she discovers that she might be the key to stopping an imminent war. Surrounded by friends ready to help her at any cost, Sophie must face an invisible enemy and secrets that could change her destiny forever.

  • Volume 4: Neverseen

    Sophie Foster and her friends are on the run. After discovering the existence of the Neverseen, a rebel group willing to do anything to spread chaos, she knows time is running out. As alliances form and break, Sophie must find a way to prevent a war that could destroy everything she loves. But the real enemy might be closer than she thinks.

  • Volume 5: Lodestar

    Sophie Foster has always known she was special, but she never imagined her power could become a weapon. As she and her friends work to thwart the Neverseen’s plans, Sophie must confront her own limits and face a moral dilemma: how far is she willing to go to save her world? The Lodestar Project might be their only hope, but at what cost?

  • Volume 6: Nightfall

    As darkness thickens around the Lost Cities, Sophie Foster fights to hold on to hope. With enemies multiplying and secrets resurfacing, she finds herself juggling her role as a protector with her own need for answers. When all seems lost, Sophie discovers that sometimes, darkness can hide the light.

  • Volume 7: Flashback

    As unlikely alliances form, Sophie Foster is confronted with memories she would rather forget. With the fate of the Lost Cities hanging in the balance, she must draw on her inner strength to face difficult truths. But some battles can only be won by accepting the loss of something precious.

  • Volume 8: Legacy

    Sophie Foster faces a heartbreaking truth: sometimes, to move forward, you have to leave the past behind. As tensions reach their peak in the Lost Cities, she must navigate between loyalty and betrayal to protect those she loves. But every choice has consequences, and some legacies cannot be ignored.
