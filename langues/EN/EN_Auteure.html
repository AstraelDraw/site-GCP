Who is the author?

Shannon Messenger is an American author, best known for her series Keeper of the Lost Cities and Sky Fall. She began writing at a young age, but it was in 2012, with the publication of the first book in the Keeper of the Lost Cities series, that she gained immense success. This young adult fantasy series stands out for its rich world, populated by magical creatures, powers, and mysteries to solve. The first book follows Sophie Foster, a teenage girl with the ability to read minds, who discovers that she belongs to a hidden world full of secrets. This series quickly captivated readers, becoming a bestseller and leading to several sequels.

Shannon Messenger's work is characterized by her boundless imagination and ability to twist plots in ways that surprise and engage her audience. She combines thrilling adventures with deep emotional dilemmas, exploring themes such as friendship, loyalty, and the search for oneself. What particularly distinguishes Keeper of the Lost Cities is how Messenger builds her world, balancing fantastic elements with universal problems that young readers can relate to.

In addition to her literary success, Shannon Messenger has built a strong community of fans, who are deeply passionate about her characters and world. She maintains a close relationship with her readers through social media interactions, where she shares her inspirations and gives glimpses of her ongoing work.
