Meet the main characters:

Sophie Foster

Keefe Sencen

Fitz Vacker

Dex Dizznee

Biana Vacker

Sophie Foster is a young girl with extraordinary abilities far beyond what she could ever imagine. She discovers that she is an elf, and her life changes when she joins the Lost Cities, a secret world where magic and mystery reign. Alongside her loyal friends, she embarks on thrilling adventures. Keefe Sencen, the mischievous and charismatic prankster, is always ready to lighten the mood with his humor, but hides an emotional depth that makes him especially endearing. Fitz Vacker, on the other hand, is the brilliant and brave boy who often serves as a guide for Sophie in this new world, even though he struggles with his own family pressures. Dex Dizznee, though sometimes underestimated, is a technological genius and a loyal friend Sophie can always rely on. Finally, Biana Vacker, Fitz's sister, transforms from a carefree girl to a courageous young woman, ready to prove her worth in decisive moments. Together, these five friends face colossal challenges, explore hidden secrets, and form an unbreakable bond where trust and friendship are essential to overcoming the trials of the Lost Cities.
